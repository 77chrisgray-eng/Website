    Welcome Home

    A church family rooted in faith, hope and love

    Jordan River Missionary Baptist Church is a community of believers in Buffalo, New York, gathered to worship God, grow in His Word, and share His love with our neighbors.

    We are a Holy Spirit–led, Bible-teaching, Bible-believing church under the leadership of Rev. Dennis L. Gray, Pastor and Founder. Come grow with us as we seek a full understanding of God’s Word.
