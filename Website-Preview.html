    Welcome Home

    A church family rooted in faith, hope and love

    Jordan River Missionary Baptist Church is a community of believers in Buffalo, New York, gathered to worship God, grow in His Word, and share His love with our neighbors.

    We are a Holy Spirit–led, Bible-teaching, Bible-believing church under the leadership of Rev. Dennis L. Gray, Pastor and Founder. Come grow with us as we seek a full understanding of God’s Word.

        Privacy

        Privacy Notice

        How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

        Information you choose to send us

        When you submit a form on this website (contact, counseling, transportation, prayer or announcements), the information you enter is sent securely over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a form-delivery service, which forwards it by email to authorized church staff. We use it only to respond to your request.

        Who can see it

        Submissions are never published on this website. Counseling and prayer requests are shared only with the pastor and authorized leaders; transportation requests only with authorized transportation coordinators.

        What we ask you not to send

        Please do not send sensitive personal, medical or financial details through website forms. You can share those privately and in person with the pastor.

        Giving

        Online gifts are processed by a third-party payment provider on its own secure page. This website never collects or stores card or bank information.

        Announcements & text messages

        We only send announcements to people who ask for them. You will never be signed up automatically. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can reply STOP to any church text message to opt out.

        Embedded services

        Some pages show content from YouTube, Google Maps and Google Calendar. Those services may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

        Questions

        Please contact the church office with any questions or to ask that we delete information you sent us.
